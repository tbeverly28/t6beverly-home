Goals

Big Picture 5+ Years

  • Have stable work/a stable job in the industry (?)
  • Lots of freelance work
  • Work in a small, tight-knit company/agency/group where my ideas have a higher change of coming to fruition

On the other hand, I really want to think about why I'm even doing this in the first place. "People don't buy what you do, they buy why you do it" - Simon Sinek.

This Ted Talk really made me do a 180 on my goals haha. Am I doing this for money? Well, yes because I will eventually have to "put food on the table", as they say, but I chose this specific field for a reason. Not because I thought I could potentially be sitting at a desk making 6+ figures doing nothing all day, but because I want to live and breathe technology, design, art, craft, nature, and culture. Whatever that means and as corny as it sounds, I want to make this a part of my everyday life. I think my 'raison d'etre' is to bring people closer to technology enough so that it doesn't feel intrusive, disruptive, or 'disgusting' (I'm looking at you AI food menus), but exciting and humane. I'm still trying to "laserfocus" this idea into something more specific, but I'm also still trying to figure things out along the way.

  • I want interacting with technology to feel like breathing. It shouldn't be something you have to put much thought into.
  • I want interacting with technology to feel memorable and exciting! Things can adhere to a set of principles/established design rules, but there's no reason those same things can't be fun to interact with.

I don't want to reinvent the wheel, just make it more adaptable to change. I'm glad I thought this through a bit more.

By the end of the week I want will...

  • be thinking about my goals more. What does it mean to "breathe" this stuff.

  • have started planning and designing the Wix portfolio project for my mom
  • have worked on improving all my previous pages using the things I learn in the new few modules

By the end of the course I want will...

  • know what looks good to me and be confidence in my decisions
  • have a least one client or professional work completed
